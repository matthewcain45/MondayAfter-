How we use your data.

The Monday After is run by Capstone Global Advisory, which is responsible for your data (the "data controller").

What we keep

  • Your email address, so we can send you each monthly file.
  • Your answers in each file, and which screens you reached, so we can see how readers respond to crises and improve each scenario.
  • Your flight recorder, encrypted. The author can't read it. You can ask for a copy to be emailed to you.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, no tracking pixels, no advertising and no third-party analytics.
  • We never sell or share your data.

Where it lives and for how long

Your data is stored with Amazon Web Services in London. We delete it 2 years after you last took part, and unconfirmed sign-ups after 7 days.

Your choices

Every email has a link to take a 3-month break, unsubscribe, or delete all your data straight away. You can also reply to any email to ask a question.
