How we use your data.

This notice explains what The Monday After keeps about you, why, where, for how long, and how you can change your mind. We've tried to keep it short and plain. If anything is unclear, reply to any of our emails and ask.

Who we are

The Monday After is run by Capstone Global Advisory, which is responsible for your data (the "data controller").

What we keep, and why

  • Your email address, so we can send you each monthly incident file, confirm that you signed up, and send anything you ask for, such as a copy of your flight recorder.
  • Your answers in each file: the choices you make, the threads you open, what you flag, your forecast, and the one thing you choose to take back (with the date you picked). We keep them so you can return and change them, so we can send you reports about your own answers in future, and so we can understand how readers respond to crises and improve each scenario.
  • Which screens you reached, and when. This shows us where a scenario works and where people give up. We record it inside our own system, never with outside analytics companies.
  • Your flight recorder (what you'd do in the first hour, day and week). It is encrypted as soon as it's saved. The service is built so that we can't read it while running it: it never appears in our spreadsheets or logs, and the only thing the system can do with it is email it to your own address when you ask.
  • Your status: when you signed up and confirmed, whether you've paused (and until when), and whether you've unsubscribed.
  • For a short time, a scrambled version of your internet address (kept for up to an hour) and of your email address (up to a day). We use these only to stop the sign-up form being misused. They can't easily be turned back into the originals.

We don't use cookies, tracking pixels, advertising or outside analytics, and we don't track whether you open our emails. While you have a file open, your browser keeps your personal link for that visit only, so the page can save your answers.

Our legal basis

to be confirmed with our data protection adviser. Proposed: your consent, given when you sign up and confirm, for the monthly emails and for keeping your answers and flight recorder; and our legitimate interest in improving the scenarios, for recording which screens readers reach

You can withdraw your consent at any time: unsubscribe or delete your data using the link in any of our emails.

Who else handles your data

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) runs the website, stores the data and sends our emails, acting only on our instructions. Your data is stored in AWS's London region. To make pages load quickly, AWS may handle your visit at its nearest location in Europe or North America.
  • Google Workspace holds our email inbox. If you reply to one of our emails, your reply is kept there.

We never sell your data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • If you don't confirm your email: we delete your sign-up after 7 days.
  • Everything else: we delete it 2 years after you last opened one of our files. Unsubscribing stops the emails; your past answers are then deleted 2 years after you last took part.
  • If you ask us to delete your data: it's removed from the service straight away. Our backups, which let us recover from mistakes, keep a copy for up to 35 days before it disappears for good.
  • Replies you send us stay in our inbox until we delete them.
  • If an email to you bounces or is marked as spam, AWS keeps your address on a "do not send" list so we don't email you again.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, limit how we use it, object to how we use it, or receive a copy to take elsewhere. You can take a 3-month break, unsubscribe, or delete everything at once using the link at the bottom of any of our emails. For anything else, reply to any email or write to the address above. We'll respond within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you by email first.
